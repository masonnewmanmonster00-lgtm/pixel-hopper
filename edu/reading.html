🔍 Reading Detective

Every story hides clues. Read short mysteries, facts and tales, answer the questions, then tap the exact sentence that proves your answer. Crack 4 cases to earn your Master Detective badge!

How to play

📄 Read the short passage. ❓ Answer the question: details, main idea, inference (reading between the lines) or what a word means in context.

🖍 Evidence: after some questions you must tap the sentence in the passage that proves the answer. Good detectives always show their proof!

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
