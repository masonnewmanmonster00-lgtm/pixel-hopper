⚖ Balance Scales

An equation is a balance! Add and remove weights to keep the scales level, discover mystery boxes, then solve one-step and two-step equations by doing the same thing to both sides.

How to play

⚖ Balance it: press the weight buttons to add to the left or right pan. Tap a weight on a pan to take it off. Press Check when the beam is level.

📦 Mystery boxes: work out what one box weighs. ✏ Equations: choose the move that keeps both sides equal (like "subtract 3 from both sides"), then type the value of x.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
