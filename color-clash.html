Rules: Match the top card by colour, number or symbol. ⊘ Skip jumps the next player, ⇄ Reverse flips direction, +2 and Wild +4 make the next player draw (or stack another on top if stacking is on). Wilds pick the colour. Can't play? Tap the deck. Down to one card? Hit LAST CARD! before you're caught (+2 penalty) — and press CATCH! when a rival forgets. Win a round to score the points left in everyone's hands.
