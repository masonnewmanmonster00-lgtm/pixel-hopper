🎲 Probability Lab

How likely is it? Place events on the chance scale, work out the odds of spinners, dice, coins and marble bags as fractions, then run real experiments and record results in tally charts.

How to play

📊 Chance scale: drag the marker from impossible to certain. 🎯 Fractions of chance: chance = favourable outcomes ÷ all outcomes.

🧪 Experiments: press Run to spin, roll or flip many times. Your tally chart fills up, then answer what the results show. Press R to run 10 trials.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
