🔤 Word Play Lab

Mix up letters, flip words backwards and climb word ladders! Unscramble anagrams with clues, find palindromes that read the same both ways, and change one letter at a time to get from CAT to DOG.

How to play

🔀 Anagrams: tap the letters in order to spell the word that matches the clue. 🪜 Word ladders: pick the next word, changing just one letter each step, until you reach the goal.

🔁 Palindromes: pick the word or phrase that reads the same forwards and backwards.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
