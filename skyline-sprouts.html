How to play

  • Build roads, then paint Residential, Commercial and Industrial zones beside them (drag to paint).
  • Zones need road access and ⚡ power to grow, and 💧 water to grow big.
  • Watch the R/C/I demand bars. Parks, police, fire and schools raise happiness and land value. Factories pollute!
  • Reach population milestones to unlock new buildings.
  • Pan: drag with ✋ tool, two fingers, right-mouse or WASD. Zoom: pinch or mouse wheel.
