🌉 Bridge Science

Become a bridge engineer! Learn about push and pull (compression and tension), discover why triangles are so strong, meet beam, arch, truss and suspension bridges, then design your own bridge and test it with a heavy truck.

How to play

🛠 Sandbox rules: tap a joint, then tap a joint next to it to add a beam (tap the same pair again to remove it). The road (the row level with the cliffs) must reach all the way across. The banks and cliff joints are fixed. Press Test to drive the truck over.

A bridge fails if a joint can wobble (add triangles!) or if a beam is pushed or pulled harder than its strength. After a test, red = squeezed (compression) and blue = stretched (tension).

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
