Ski Jump

🎿 SKI JUMP

Time your take-off, balance your V-style against the wind and nail the telemark. Five hills from the Normal Hill to monster ski flying.

How to jump

Gate: tap or press Space to push off.

Take-off: tap / Space as you reach the green zone at the end of the table. Late or early costs height and style.

Flight: your body tips up on its own and gusts push you around. Hold (left side of screen, Space or ↑) to lean forward, release to rise. Keep the posture needle in the green band for the best lift.

Landing: hit LAND (right side button, ↓ or L) just before you touch down for a telemark. Too early kills your lift; no telemark costs style points; landing far past HS on the flat is dangerous.

Score = distance points (K-point is worth 60, or 120 on flying hills) + 3 middle judges of 5 + wind compensation. P pauses, R retries in Training, M mutes.
