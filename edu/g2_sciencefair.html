🧪 Science Fair Lab

Think like a scientist! Ask a testable question, write a hypothesis, choose what to change, measure and keep the same, set up a fair test, then read your results and draw a conclusion.

How to play

⚖ Fair test: make Setup B match Setup A in every way except the one thing you are testing (tap B%HOW%#39;s values to change them), then press Check. 🗂 Variables: sort each into changed, measured or kept the same.

📊 Results: read the data and choose the conclusion it supports.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
