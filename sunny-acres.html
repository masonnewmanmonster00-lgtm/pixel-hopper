Sunny Acres

A cozy little farm of your own

How to play: Tap/click a tile to walk there and use your tool. ✋ Auto does the right thing: till grass, plant the chosen seed, water, harvest. Crops grow in real time only while watered — rain waters for you. Sell in the 🏪 Shop (tap the stall), buy chickens & cows, expand your land and upgrade tools. Sleep in the house to skip the night. Seasons change every 7 days; out-of-season crops wither.
PC: WASD/arrows move · Space/E use tool on facing tile · 1–6 tools · Q/R change seed · B shop · Z sleep · M mute
