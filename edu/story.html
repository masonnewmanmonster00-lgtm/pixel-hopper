📚 Story Builder

Become an author! Put story events in order, find the beginning, middle and end, choose vivid words and smart connectives, then build your own stories and hear them read back.

How to play

🔀 Story order: tap the events in the order they happen. 🧭 Beginning, middle or end: decide where a sentence belongs.

✨ Word Wizard: pick the most vivid adjective or verb. 🔗 Connectives join ideas: pick or tap the best one.

✍ Author Studio: choose a character, setting, problem and ending to build a story. Press 🔊 to hear it, and find it later in 📖 My Stories.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
