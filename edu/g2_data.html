Data Detective

🔍 Data Detective

Crack the case with numbers! Build dot plots, level towers to find the mean, sort data to find the median, spot the mode, measure the range and sniff out outliers in real-world style data.

How to play

📍 Dot plots: drag a dot from the tray onto a number (or tap the column) to add it; tap a dot to remove it. 🧱 Level the towers: tap a tall tower, then a short one, to move a cube until every tower is equal: that height is the mean.

🔢 Sort: tap numbers smallest to largest. ⌨ Keypad: type answers. Wrong answers show the working.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
