🏎 Rounding Rally

Race to the nearest ten, hundred or thousand! Drag numbers onto the track, see which side of the halfway flag they land on, estimate sums and products, and check whether answers are reasonable.

How to play

🏁 Number line: drag the car to the number. The 🚩 flag marks halfway. Left of it rounds down; on or past it rounds up.

Rule: look at the digit to the right of the place you round to. 0–4 round down, 5–9 round up. 🤔 Reasonable? Estimate first, then compare.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
