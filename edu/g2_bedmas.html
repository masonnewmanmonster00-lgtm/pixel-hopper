🧮 Operation Order

Brackets, Exponents, Division and Multiplication, Addition and Subtraction! Solve expressions one step at a time in the right order, and spot where a classmate%BLURB%#39;s working went wrong.

How to play

👆 Step it out: tap the operation that must happen next. It collapses into its answer until one number is left. ⌨ Solve: type the final answer.

🔎 Find the mistake: pick the first wrong step. BEDMAS: Brackets → Exponents → × and ÷ left to right → + and − left to right.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
