Command your armies. Conquer the world.
Opponents
Difficulty
AI speed
How to play: 42 territories, 6 continents. Each turn: Reinforce (tap your lands to place armies; hold whole continents for bonus armies), Attack (tap your land, then an enemy neighbour — Roll or Auto), then Fortify (move armies once between connected lands). Conquer a land in a turn to earn a card; trade 3 matching or 3 different cards for extra armies. Eliminate every rival to win! On a phone, drag the map sideways to scroll.
