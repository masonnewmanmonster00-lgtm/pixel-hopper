🏁 Decimal Dash

Race through tenths, hundredths and percents! Drag markers onto number lines, shade grids, compare and order decimals, switch between fractions, decimals and percents, and solve percent-of problems.

How to play

📍 Number lines: drag the marker (or use arrow keys) to the decimal. 🟧 Grids: tap or drag across squares to shade them; each strip is a tenth, each small square a hundredth.

⚖ Compare %HOW% order decimals, 🔁 convert fractions, decimals and percents, and work out % of amounts on the number pad.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
