💡 Inventor's Workshop

Step into the workshop! Put famous inventions on a timeline, match problems to the inventions that solved them, try hands-on "how it works" challenges, and learn the design process real inventors use.

How to play

🕰 Timeline: tap inventions from oldest to newest. 🔌 Circuit: tap each gap to connect it and close the switch, then check if the bulb lights. 🔀 Steps: tap steps in order.

🧩 Match: pick the invention that solves the problem. Most inventions were built by many people improving each other%HOW%#39;s ideas, so the game tells those stories carefully.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
