🧭 Habitat Explorer

Explore five amazing habitats: desert, ocean, Arctic, grassland and mountain! Match adaptations to the places they help, design your own creature for a habitat, and figure out why animals would struggle somewhere else.

How to play

🗂 Sort: tap an adaptation, then tap the habitat it helps in. 🧬 Design a creature: choose one feature in each row that suits the habitat, then press Check.

🤔 Why would it struggle? pick the best reason. Wrong answers explain the science.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
