Orbit Golf

⛳ ORBIT GOLF

Golf across the solar system. Bend your shots around planets, dodge black holes, ride moons and wormholes, and sink it in as few strokes as you can.

How to play

Aim: drag anywhere and pull back like a slingshot — the further you pull, the harder the shot. Let go to launch. The dotted line previews the first part of the flight.

Keyboard: ← → aim (Shift for fine), ↑ ↓ power, Space to shoot. R restarts the hole, P pauses, M mutes.

Planets and moons pull the ball in — the ball can land on them and you play from where it stops. Black holes swallow it (+1 penalty), so do flying off into deep space. Wormholes link in pairs of the same color. Moons only move while the ball is flying.
