🌱 Savings Sprout

Grow your money tree! Set savings goals, sort needs from wants, split money into Save, Spend and Share jars, and watch simple interest help your savings grow.

How to play

🎯 Goals: work out how many weeks it takes to save up. 🗂 Sorting: tap an item, then tap Need or Want.

🫙 Jars: split money into Save, Spend and Share. 📈 Interest: type the answer and press Check.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
