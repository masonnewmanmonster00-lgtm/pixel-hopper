🧱 Paragraph Builder

Build strong paragraphs like a burger: a topic sentence on top, juicy supporting details in the middle, and a concluding sentence to finish. Choose transition words and kick out the sentence that does not belong.

How to play

🔀 Build: tap sentences in order: topic sentence, details, then the conclusion. 🚫 Odd one out: tap the sentence that does not belong.

✅ Choose: pick the best topic sentence, transition word or conclusion. Wrong answers explain why.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
