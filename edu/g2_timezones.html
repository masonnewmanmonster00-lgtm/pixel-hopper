🌍 Time Zone Trek

When it is lunchtime in London, is Tokyo asleep? Learn why the world has time zones, slide clocks across a 24-hour timeline, convert times between cities and plan calls around the planet.

How to play

Earth turns 360° in 24 hours, so every 15° of longitude is about 1 hour. Places east are ahead; places west are behind.

🕒 Timeline: drag the marker (or use arrow keys) to the right time. Offsets here use standard time (no daylight saving) and UTC (London in winter).

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
