📖 Dictionary Detective

Become a dictionary pro! Put words in alphabetical order down to the 2nd and 3rd letter, use guide words to find the right page, pick the right meaning of tricky words, read parts of speech and clap out syllables.

How to play

🔤 ABC order: tap the words in alphabetical order. 📖 Guide words: decide if a word belongs on the dictionary page shown.

🧩 Meanings, parts of speech and syllables: pick the best answer. Wrong answers explain how a dictionary helps.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
