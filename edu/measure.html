📏 Measure Up

Rulers, scales, measuring jugs and conversions! Read instruments, drag to measure out exact amounts, convert metric and imperial units and become an estimation expert across 6 worlds.

How to play

👀 Read it: look at the ruler, scale or jug and type the amount on the number pad.

✋ Set it: drag the pencil, needle or water level (or use the arrow keys) to the target, then press Check.

🔁 Convert between units and 🤔 estimate sensible amounts for real objects.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
