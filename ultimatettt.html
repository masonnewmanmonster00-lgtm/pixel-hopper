How to play

  • Classic: get three in a row on the 3×3 grid.
  • Ultimate: the big board is 9 small boards. Win a small board by getting 3 in a row in it; win the game with 3 small boards in a row.
  • The cell you pick sends your opponent to the matching small board (top-right cell → top-right board). Active boards glow.
  • If you're sent to a board that's already won or full, you may play anywhere open.
  • Controls: tap/click a cell, or arrows + Space/Enter. U undo (2-player), P pause, R restart, Esc menu.
