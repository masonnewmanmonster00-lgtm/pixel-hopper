DEPTHS OF
EMBERFALL

A turn-based dungeon crawl. One life. How deep can you go?

HOW TO PLAY

• Move/attack: Arrows or WASD (walk into enemies to fight). Phones: swipe, tap a side of the screen, or use the D-pad.
• Wait: Space or .   Bag: I   Descend: Enter or > on the stairs.
• Items are picked up when you step on them. Equip weapons and armor, drink potions, read scrolls.
• Every 5th floor a boss guards the way down — the stairs appear when it dies.
• Death is permanent. Your deepest floor is remembered.
