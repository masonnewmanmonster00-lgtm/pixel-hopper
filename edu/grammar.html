📜 GRAMMAR QUEST

Journey through five lands, from the Word Meadow to Dragon Peak! Sort words, fix punctuation, build sentences and spot sneaky mistakes to earn stars and wake the sleeping dragon.

How to play

Each level has 8 challenges. Get them right on the first try for the most stars. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see a friendly explanation so you learn why.

🏷 Word Sort: what part of speech is the yellow word? ✒ Punctuation Fix: pick the sentence that is written correctly. 🧱 Sentence Builder: tap word tiles in order. 🔍 Spot the Mistake: tap the one wrong word.

Keys: 1–6 choose an answer, Enter check / next, Backspace undo a tile, M mute.
