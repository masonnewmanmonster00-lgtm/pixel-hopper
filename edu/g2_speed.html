🏁 Speed Race

Ready, set, GO! Watch animated races, use the speed triangle to find speed, distance and time, read distance-time graphs, get a feel for km/h and m/s, and decide who is really fastest.

How to play

🏁 Races: watch the racers (press ↻ to replay), then decide who is faster. 🔺 Triangle: cover what you want: speed = distance ÷ time, distance = speed × time, time = distance ÷ speed.

📈 Graphs: steeper line = faster; flat line = stopped. Type answers on the keypad. Wrong answers show the working.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
