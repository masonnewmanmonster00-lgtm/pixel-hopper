How to play

PC: A/D move · W/Space jump (hold in water to swim) · Left mouse: mine / attack · Right mouse: place block / use item · 1–0 or wheel: hotbar · E: inventory & crafting · Esc: pause
Phone: joystick moves (push up to jump), ⤒ jumps, ⛏/🧱 switches between mining & placing, then tap or hold on a block. 🎒 opens inventory.
Getting started: punch trees for logs → craft planks → workbench → wooden pickaxe → stone tools → furnace (smelt ore) → iron & diamond gear.
Torches light caves (coal + planks). Zombies roam at night, bats and slimes lurk in caves. Craft a Glowing Eye and use it at night to summon the boss — it also comes on its own on night 3!
Armor in your inventory is worn automatically. The world autosaves every minute.
