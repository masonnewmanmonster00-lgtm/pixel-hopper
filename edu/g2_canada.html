🍁 Canada Quest

Explore Canada from coast to coast to coast! Find all 10 provinces and 3 territories on the map, learn their capitals, match famous landmarks, sort them into regions and collect fun facts.

How to play

🗺 Map: tap the province or territory asked for (the map is simplified, not to exact scale). 🏛 Capitals and 📍 landmarks: pick the right answer.

🧭 Regions: tap a place, then tap its region. Wrong answers show the right spot and a fact.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
