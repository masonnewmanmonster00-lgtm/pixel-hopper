🎭 Figure It Out

Words can paint pictures! Tell similes from metaphors, catch personification, hyperbole and onomatopoeia, discover what idioms REALLY mean (not what they literally say), and spot figurative language hiding in poems.

How to play

🗂 Sort: tap a sentence, then tap its group. 🔍 Spot it: tap the highlighted phrase in the poem or passage that uses the device asked for.

🖼 Idioms: compare the silly literal picture with the real meaning and choose correctly. Wrong answers explain the clue.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
