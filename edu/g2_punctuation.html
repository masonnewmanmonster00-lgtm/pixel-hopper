🚓 Punctuation Patrol

Join the Punctuation Patrol! Choose the right end marks, put commas in lists and after introductory words, sort contractions from possessives (its or it%BLURB%#39;s?), punctuate dialogue with quotation marks and fix whole sentences.

How to play

🔘 Slot fixes: tap the little boxes between words to cycle through punctuation marks until the sentence is correct, then press Check.

✅ Choices: pick the correctly punctuated answer. Wrong answers explain the rule.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
