🪶 Poetry Forge

Forge your own poems! Count syllables for haiku (5-7-5) and cinquains (2-4-6-8-2), find rhyme schemes like AABBA in limericks, build acrostics that spell a word, and spot each form in short poems.

How to play

🔀 Build: tap lines in order to make the poem. 👏 Syllables: clap and type how many syllables a line has.

🎵 Rhyme schemes: lines that rhyme get the same letter: AABBA, ABAB and so on. Wrong answers show the counting.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
