🧩 Pattern Detective

Patterns are everywhere! Crack shape and colour codes, find missing numbers, discover the hidden rule and predict how growing patterns will grow. 5 cases, 15 levels.

How to play

🔮 What comes next? pick the next item. 🧱 Extend it: tap shapes in order to continue the pattern. 🔢 Missing term: type the missing number on the pad.

🕵 Find the rule: choose the rule that makes the pattern. 🌱 Growing patterns: study figures 1, 2, 3 and predict a later figure.

Get a level right with no mistakes for ★★★. A wrong answer is fine: you'll see why, so you learn something every time.

Keys: 1–6 choose, Enter check / next, M mute.
